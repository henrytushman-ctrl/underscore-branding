CHANGELOG] -------------------------------------------------------------------- 1. WHAT THIS IS -------------------------------------------------------------------- Underscore is a community with one working part: people give time before anyone asks them to. No feed. No points. No algorithm deciding who matters today. There is a line, and there are people on it. An hour of your experience costs you almost nothing — you already paid for it the hard way. To someone stuck where you once were, that hour is worth a month. We route those hours. That's the product. This file is the whole website. We figured the words should be able to carry it. If they can't, no amount of gradient was going to save us. -------------------------------------------------------------------- 2. THE CORE -------------------------------------------------------------------- The member community is called the Core: builders, operators, and researchers, mid-something, in 40 cities. The Core runs on three conventions: 1. give first, ask later 2. the ask is a contribution (your question becomes the next person's search result) 3. nobody keeps score; everybody keeps showing up Every chapter runs an open table. The only agenda item, every week, in every city: who needs what, and who's got it. -------------------------------------------------------------------- 3. HOW GIVING WORKS -------------------------------------------------------------------- A good ask is specific, honest about what you've tried, and easy to say no to. Example of a real one: "Does anyone actually understand injection-mold tooling? I'm out of my depth and my quotes are 3x budget." What happened next: Tom (Detroit) redesigned the tool count from 4 to 2 on a Tuesday call. Jonas (Berlin) opened a Stuttgart shop's door. Eleven days from ask to purchase order. Nobody invoiced anybody. When it works for you, you do it for the next person. That is the entire repayment schedule. -------------------------------------------------------------------- 5.
