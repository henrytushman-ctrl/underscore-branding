What this is

Underscore is a community of builders, operators, and experts who give time to each other — first, and without keeping score.

The underscore is the character you type before the thing that matters. It holds a place. It carries weight. It never asks for credit. That's the posture of every member here.

No content feed. No badge wall. Just 1,200+ people across 40 cities who pick up when another member calls.

Three principles

  • Give first. Help arrives before the ask is finished. Nobody tracks favors.
  • Stay plain. No titles at the door, no performance inside. What you know matters; what you're called doesn't.
  • Connect sideways. The best answer usually lives in a different industry, a different city, a different life. We make that call happen.
