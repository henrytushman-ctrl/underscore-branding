02 · THE PROBLEM

Building anything real is mostly done alone.

The hard questions land at 11pm. The person who has solved your exact problem exists — somewhere — and you have no way to reach them. Networking events don't fix this. Follower counts don't fix this.

03 · THE IDEA

So we made one rule:
give an hour before you ask for one.

Underscore is a community of builders, operators and experts who put their time in first. No leaderboard. No clout. Just a standing answer to the question: who's got an hour for this?

06 · HOW GIVING WORKS

Three steps. No ceremony.

STEP_01

Someone asks.

A real question from real work — a contract, a tool, a hire, a wall they've hit. Posted plainly to the Core.

STEP_02

Someone who knows shows up.

Usually within a day. A reply, a call, a working session. Time, not links.

STEP_03

The favor moves forward.

You don't pay it back. You pay it down the line, to the next person stuck where you were.
