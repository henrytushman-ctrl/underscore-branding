Manifesto

The line holds the place.

Every builder hits the blank: the bug nobody documented, the price nobody publishes, the decision nobody can make for you.

Underscore exists so the blank is never empty. Members give hours, not advice from a distance. We answer, we show our work, we leave the door open behind us.
