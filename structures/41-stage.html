SCENE 01 — THE LINE
Scene 01 — The line

Start with a single line.

An underscore is what a page shows you before the answer arrives. A held space. Unfinished, on purpose.

Underscore is a community built on that posture. We are people who build things — companies, products, machines, research — and who keep room open for whoever is building next to us.

Scene 02 — The crowd

Since 2021, one line has become many.

We call the people behind them the Core: builders, operators, and experts who give their time to each other. No gurus, no stage, no pitch at the end. Just people who answer when you ask.

Scene 04 — The map

Forty cities so far.

The map doesn't show a headquarters because there isn't one. Wherever a member picks up a call at midnight — Oakland, Beirut, Seoul, Detroit — that's an office.

Scene 05 — The ledger

We count one thing.

9,000 hours given, member to member, since 2021. More than 1,200 people across 40 cities. Time is the only dues, and the ledger only goes up.
