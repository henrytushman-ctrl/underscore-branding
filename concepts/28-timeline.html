Underscore — est. 2021

Five years of showing up.

Underscore is a community built on one habit: when someone asks, someone answers. This is the record of where that habit has taken us.

2021 — eight people in a borrowed room.

The line so far.

No pivots, no rebrands, no era of growth-at-any-cost. Just entries. Each one happened because a member gave time they didn't have to spare.

2021

Eight people, one rule. Give first, ask later. Maya hosted; nobody took minutes. The rule held.

2022

First chapter outside the US. Berlin, because Jonas wouldn't stop asking.

2023

The hours ledger opens. 1,000 hours given by June. Nobody invoiced anybody. We checked.

2024

Forty cities. First Core summit on Tom's factory floor in Detroit. Folding chairs, real talk.

2025

9,000 hours given. Leila's research group in Beirut becomes the model for every chapter after it.

2026

Still here. 1,200 members and the same one rule we started with.
