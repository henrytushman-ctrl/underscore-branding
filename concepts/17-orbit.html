How the Core works

There is no platform to learn and no ladder to climb. Three commitments, kept plainly, hold the whole thing together.

01

Give first

Every member offers something standing — an hour, a skill, an open door — before they ever make a request. The Core gives first and asks later.

02

Stay reachable

A member who writes to another member gets an answer. Not always yes — but always an answer, from a person, in plain language.

03

Keep no score

There are no credits, points, or tiers. 9,000 hours have been given since 2021, and nobody is owed a single one of them.
