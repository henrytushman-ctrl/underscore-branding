Built in Common

Underscore is a community that treats generosity as infrastructure. Time given freely is the foundation everything else rests on.

The Charter

Five articles. Written in 2021 by eight people; unamended since, because nothing has needed amending.

Article I

Time is the only dues. No member pays money to belong; every member gives hours.

Article II

Give first. Help is offered before terms are discussed, and terms are rarely discussed.

Article III

Asking is a contribution. A clear request gives someone else the chance to be useful.

Article IV

What is built here is held in common. Knowledge shared inside the Core belongs to the Core.

Article V

The door stays open. Anyone willing to live by these articles may add their name.
