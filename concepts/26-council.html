Underscore · The Council · Convened 2021

Good company is infrastructure.

Roads, power, water — and the people who will pick up when it matters. Underscore maintains the last one. A standing council of builders, operators, and experts called the Core, who give their time to each other as a matter of course.

01 — What the Core does

Three duties. No ceremony.

i.

Answers, within the day

A pricing question, a hiring doubt, a contract clause that smells wrong. You ask the Core. Someone who has lived it answers before the day ends. That's the standard, not the exception.

ii.

Hours, given first

Members commit real working hours to each other — reviews, intros, a week on a shop floor. Nine thousand hours and counting, none of them billed. The Core gives first and asks later.

iii.

Company, kept

Building is long and mostly quiet. The Core keeps a seat warm in forty cities — a standing table where your work is taken seriously and your name is known.
