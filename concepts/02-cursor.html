Start before you're ready

Ready is a horizon, not a place. The cursor doesn't wait for confidence, and confidence usually shows up a few lines in anyway.

02
Plain words travel further

Jargon is a toll booth. We write like we'd talk to a smart friend at a kitchen table, because that's who we're building for.

Leave space for someone else

The underscore joins words_like_this. Everything we make leaves a blank for the next person to fill — that's generosity with a keyboard.
