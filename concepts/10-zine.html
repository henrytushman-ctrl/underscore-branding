stuff we believe.

the community board.

tuesday writing circle

six of us, one shared doc, zero judgement. bring a sentence you're scared of. leave with a paragraph you're proud of.

ship-it saturdays

everyone launches something tiny before lunch. a poem, a webpage, a sourdough. tiny counts. tiny is the point.

looking for: a co-plotter

marta has half a board game and full enthusiasm. she needs someone who owns dice and opinions. that's it. that's the ad.

the answer hour

thursdays, the old hands sit in a channel and answer anything. no question too small. especially the small ones.

first-word fund

a small pot of money for anyone starting their first anything. no application form. just tell us what you'll begin.

show & tell, all-ages

last friday a retired electrician taught us circuit bending and a nine-year-old taught us patience. fair trade.

the part where you join

your line is waiting.

no fee, no form longer than your name, no "onboarding journey." you show up, we make room. that's the whole machine.
