Underscore From the desk of the Core
Re: An hour of your time June 2026

Dear builder,

You don't know us yet, so we'll keep this plain. Underscore is a network of people who make things — companies, machines, software, research — and who give their time to each other. The members call this network the Core. There are about 1,200 of us, in 40 cities, and since 2021 we have given one another some nine thousand hours.

Our name is the mark that joins words. We chose it because it does its work below the line — holding things together without asking to be noticed. That is, more or less, the job description of a good member.

We should tell you what membership actually means, because it is not a badge and it is not a feed. When you join, nobody asks you for anything up front. The Core gives first and asks later. What members give looks like this:

  1. An hour a month, to whoever asks first. Founders preparing for a raise, engineers stuck on a problem, operators staring at a hiring plan.
  2. An honest read — of the deck, the contract, the prototype, the difficult email. Honest meaning honest.
  3. A name and a number, when an introduction will do more good than an opinion.
  4. A bed, a desk, or a workshop, in forty cities, when the work takes you somewhere new.

There is no leaderboard for any of this and no ledger of favors owed. It works because the people we admit are the kind who would do it anyway; we just put them in the same room. Five years on, we see no reason it should stop working.

If you build things, and you can give an hour, the rest of this letter is yours to finish.

With time to give,— the Core
