What we believe

Three things, said plainly. We don't have a manifesto. We have a garden.

Begin badly.

Every great thing starts as a blank — then a scribble, then a seedling. The first draft doesn't have to be good. It has to exist.

Leave room.

Whitespace isn't empty — it's where the next thing goes. We give more than we take, and we never crowd a good idea.

Say it plainly.

If a sentence needs a glossary, it isn't finished. No jargon, no gloss — just words that mean what they say.

Good things grow toward each other

The underscore is how words hold hands: like_this. It's also how people do.

Five strangers, five blank lines. Give them a season and you can't tell where one garden ends and the next begins. That's the whole idea.

Ready to start from scratch?

Scratch is where everything starts. Tell us what you want to grow — a project, a practice, a tiny impossible plan — and we'll hold the ground steady while it takes root.
