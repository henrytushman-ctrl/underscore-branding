Why we exist

An honest note from the editors

There is a kind of attention that has gone slightly out of fashion: the slow kind. The kind you give a sentence when you read it twice, reach for a pencil, and draw a line under it so your future self will know it mattered. Underscore is a magazine built entirely out of that gesture.

We don't chase the loudest story of the day. The loud stories have plenty of help. We go looking for the quiet ones — the night-shift nurse, the last phone booth, the friend who always holds the door — and we give them the long, careful sentences they deserve.

"We don't shout. We underline." — The masthead, in its entirety

The underscore is a humble mark. It sits below the line, asks for nothing, and makes everything above it easier to find later. That is roughly our ambition as a publication. We are not trying to be the main text of your life. We would like to be the line beneath it — the thing that says: this part, remember this part.

Every issue is free to read, because we think generosity is a better business model than urgency. If you like what we do, subscribe, share it, or write us a letter. We read all of them, usually twice, usually with a pencil.
