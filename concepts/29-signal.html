On air

What moves along the line.

Three kinds of traffic, all of it human. Nothing is ranked, promoted, or monetized. If it's on the line, a member put it there.

RX — incoming

Asks

A member says what they need, plainly. A factory contact in Gdańsk. An hour with someone who has shipped hardware. Asks are answered, not judged.

TX — outgoing

Offers

Time given before it's requested. Office hours, code reviews, a spare desk in Seoul. Most of the 9,000 hours on our ledger started as an offer.

RELAY

Intros

Two members who should know each other, connected by a third who vouches for both. The strongest signal we carry.

In plain terms

Anyone can broadcast.

There's no hierarchy of senders and receivers. Membership is the antenna; generosity is the power supply. Here is the whole protocol.

01

Give an hour

Every member commits time, not money. An hour of real attention — reviewing, introducing, advising — is the only dues we collect.

02

Ask without apology

When you need something, say so. Clear asks travel furthest. The line exists so that needing help is normal, not notable.

03

Keep the line open

Answer when you can. Pass it on when you can't. The signal stays strong because nobody hoards it.
